# Sony α7 V product landing page

## Build
- Replace the blank home page with a premium, long-form Sony α7 V product experience inspired by Apple’s product storytelling rhythm, without copying Apple branding or wording.
- Lead with an immersive camera-first opening, concise positioning, an official product link, and a restrained sticky product bar.
- Add cinematic feature chapters for the 33 MP partially stacked sensor, BIONZ XR2 AI processing, 30 fps blackout-free shooting, 16-stop dynamic range, and 4K 120p video.
- Add an editorial specifications band and a strong closing purchase prompt linking to Sony UK.

## Visual direction
- Sony Alpha character: deep near-black surfaces, warm neutral highlights, crisp white type, and a restrained copper-red accent.
- Oversized typography, generous negative space, full-width photography, edge-to-edge sections, and subtle scroll reveals.
- Use high-quality generated product and sample-scene imagery so the page is visually complete and self-contained.
- Responsive layouts for wide screens and phones, with readable type and stable media proportions.

## Technical details
- Define semantic colour, typography, motion, and surface tokens in the global design system.
- Implement the page in the existing TanStack route and add unique title, description, Open Graph, and Twitter metadata.
- Keep interactions lightweight: sticky navigation, smooth anchor movement, progressive image reveals, and reduced-motion support.
- Verify the finished page visually at desktop and mobile widths and confirm the preview builds cleanly.
